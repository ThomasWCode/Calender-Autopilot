Outlook 5.2638.1 time picker, as the app's accessibility service sees it (2026-10-05).

`uiautomator dump` shows nothing under #protection_layout on this screen (with or without
--windows); the service, which runs with flagIncludeNotImportantViews, sees the whole picker.
These come from the debug build's DEBUG_DUMP broadcast, whose dump blanks texts outside the
calendar to their lengths ('<10 chars>'), so the texts are filled in from the screenshots:
time_picker_drag_mode.png, time_picker_wheels_start.png, time_picker_wheels_end.png.

The picker opens in DRAG mode every time (it did not remember the wheel mode). One click on
#action_picker_mode switches to the WHEEL mode ("Choose Time"). #action_done applies.

Observed behaviour:
- Tapping the centre value did not open a keyboard: the wheels are stepped, not typed
  (ACTION_SET_TEXT on #numberpicker_input is still worth one try when implementing).
- Tapping the "next" row moves a wheel by one (hour 8 -> 9).
- Changing the start moves the end with it: start 08:05 -> 09:05 made the end 09:00 -> 10:00
  (duration kept). So normally only the start needs setting; the end is checked afterwards.
- Texts read as lengths were matched against the screenshots ('Choose Time' is 11 characters,
  'Mon 12 Oct' 10). The X's description (11 characters) and #action_picker_mode's (18) were not
  seen in clear: select them by position/id, not by those texts.
- The NumberPickers report bounds [0,0][0,0] and [offscreen]; their child Buttons and EditText
  have real bounds. NumberPicker offers ACTION_SCROLL_FORWARD / ACTION_SCROLL_BACKWARD.
